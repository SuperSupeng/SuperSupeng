# Darren Su (SuperSupeng)

**Founder of [AGI Villa](https://agivilla.feishu.cn/wiki/ArG4wXniVi5A2lkAIf9coosLnKc?from=from_copylink) · [Datawhale](https://github.com/datawhalechina) Contributor · AI Builder · Open-source Community Builder**

I build **AI agents, digital organizations, and AI-native products** that help builders turn ideas into working products.

我专注于 **AI Agent、数字组织、AI 原生产品与开源社区建设**，帮助 Builder 将想法快速转化为可以验证和持续增长的产品。

Based in Shanghai, China.

## What I Build

- **AI agents and digital organizations** — multi-agent collaboration, memory, delegation, and human-in-the-loop systems
- **AI-native product development** — turning early ideas into deployed products through rapid validation and iteration
- **Vibe coding education** — helping non-technical creators and independent developers build real AI applications
- **AI + hardware interaction** — exploring new interfaces between artificial intelligence and the physical world
- **Builder communities** — community-led execution, open-source collaboration, and global growth

## Datawhale & Open-source Community

My open-source journey is closely connected with **[Datawhale](https://github.com/datawhalechina)**, a community focused on open-source AI learning and collaboration.

我长期参与 Datawhale 开源社区建设，围绕 **开源项目治理、组队学习、AI 教育内容和社区协作** 推动项目落地。

Selected Datawhale work:

- **[DOPMC](https://github.com/datawhalechina/DOPMC)** — contributing to open-source project governance and the Datawhale Open-source Project Management Committee
- **[Team Learning](https://github.com/datawhalechina/team-learning)** — supporting community-led, cohort-based learning programs
- **[Hire Your AI](https://github.com/SuperSupeng/hire-your-ai)** — content initiator of an open-source Datawhale learning program for building digital coworkers and AI agent teams

This experience shaped how I approach AI education, community governance, contributor growth, and open-source project incubation.
